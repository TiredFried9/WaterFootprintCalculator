8 Ways to Reduce Your Water Usage

Here are some practical ways to conserve water in Metro Vancouver.

1. Take Shorter Showers

Reduce shower time from 20 minutes to 15 minutes. Install a water-efficient showerhead to save even more water.

2. Turn Off Taps When Not in Use

Turn off the faucet while brushing your teeth, shaving, or washing your face.

3. Use Water-Efficient Toilets

Install dual-flush or low-flow toilets and avoid unnecessary flushing.

4. Wash Full Loads of Laundry

Run the washing machine with full loads, or choose the appropriate water-level setting for smaller loads.

5. Save Water While Washing Dishes

Run the dishwasher with full loads. When washing by hand, fill the sink rather than leaving the tap running.

6. Fix Leaking Faucets and Toilets

Check for dripping taps, running toilets, and leaking pipes. Repair leaks promptly.

7. Collect Rainwater for Gardening

Use a rain barrel to collect water for outdoor plants where permitted.

8. Reduce Outdoor Watering

Use drought-tolerant plants, mulch, and drip irrigation. Follow seasonal lawn-watering restrictions.
