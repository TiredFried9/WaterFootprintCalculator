8 Ways to Reduce Your Water Usage

Here are some practical ways to reduce your daily water consumption and help conserve water in Metro Vancouver.

1. Take Shorter Showers

Reduce shower time from 20 minutes to 15 minutes. Install a water-efficient showerhead to reduce water consumption further.

2. Turn Off Taps When Not in Use

Turn off the faucet while brushing your teeth, shaving, or washing your face instead of leaving water running.

3. Use Water-Efficient Toilets

Install dual-flush or low-flow toilets and avoid unnecessary flushing.

4. Wash Full Loads of Laundry

Run the washing machine only when you have a full load, or use the appropriate water-level setting for smaller loads.

5. Save Water While Washing Dishes

Run the dishwasher with full loads. When washing by hand, fill the sink with water instead of continuously running the tap.

6. Fix Leaking Faucets and Toilets

Check for dripping taps, running toilets, and leaking pipes. Repairing leaks prevents unnecessary water waste.

7. Collect Rainwater for Gardening

Use a rain barrel to collect rainwater for watering outdoor plants instead of using treated drinking water.

8. Reduce Outdoor Watering

Use drought-tolerant plants, mulch, and drip irrigation. Follow Metro Vancouver's seasonal lawn-watering restrictions.
